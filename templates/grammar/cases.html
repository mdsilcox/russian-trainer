Adjectives and agreement

An adjective agrees with its noun in gender, number and case. Pick the row by the adjective's ending (hard, soft, after ш ж ч щ, or stressed -ой), then read down to the case. The full tables are in each case section above ({% for c in cases %}{{ c.name }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}).

  • {{ "Masculine accusative: **animate** uses the genitive form (**но'вого** студе'нта), **inanimate** copies the nominative (**но'вый** биле'т)." | rich }}
  • {{ "Feminine instrumental may be written -ою/-ею in literary style; **-ой/-ей** is standard." | rich }}
  • {{ "Stress stays on the stem in **но'вый**, **си'ний**, **хоро'ший**; in **большо'й** it sits on the ending (and the spelling rule gives **больши'м**, **больши'е**)." | rich }}

Pronouns after prepositions

{{ "**He, she, they** (его', её, их; ему', ей, им; им, ей, и'ми) add **н-** after a preposition: **у него'**, **к ней**, **с ни'ми**, **о нём**. The prepositional forms **нём, ней, них** always have it. First and second person never change: **у меня'**, **к тебе'**, **с на'ми**." | rich }}
