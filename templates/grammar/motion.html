The pairs

{{ "Однонаправленные и разнонаправленные" | ru_text }}

The unidirectional verb is one trip in one direction, happening now. The multidirectional verb is habit, round trips, ability, or movement with no fixed direction. Perfective по- is built on the unidirectional verb.

Prefixes

{{ "Приставки" | ru_text }}

A prefix adds the direction. Learn them in this order: по-, при-/у-, в-/вы-, пере-, за-, до-, про-, под-/от-, об-. They are shown here on the stem -йти́ / -ходи́ть / -е́хать / -езжа́ть.

Aspect of prefixed verbs

{{ "Вид с приставками" | ru_text }}

  • {{ "A prefix on the **unidirectional** verb makes a **perfective**: [[идти' → прийти']], [[е'хать → прие'хать]], [[лете'ть → прилете'ть]]." | rich }}
  • {{ "A prefix on the **multidirectional** verb makes the **imperfective** partner: [[приходи'ть]], [[приезжа'ть]], [[прилета'ть]]." | rich }}
  • {{ "**-ехать → -езжать** and **-ходить** are the imperfective stems for riding and walking: [[уезжа'ть]], [[уходи'ть]]." | rich }}
  • {{ "With **вы-** the perfective is stressed on the prefix: [[вы'йти]], [[вы'ехать]], against imperfective [[выходи'ть]], [[выезжа'ть]]." | rich }}
  • {{ "Past tense of the perfective means **arrived / left**, with the result in place: [[Мы прие'хали.]] (We are here.) The imperfective counts habits or the fact: [[Мы ча'сто приезжа'ли.]]" | rich }}
